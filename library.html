An Educational Library for Every Stage of Learning

MASTERBOOK is a digital educational platform designed to make learning materials, revision resources, coursebooks and educational content accessible to learners, teachers and the wider public. The MASTERBOOK Digital Library brings together resources for primary education, junior school, senior school, university education and professional learning.

The Library supports learners following the Kenyan education pathway, including Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) learning materials and subject resources for different education levels. It also provides a space for university-level academic resources, independent study and professional development materials.

Whether you are preparing for classroom learning, revising a subject, developing teaching materials or pursuing further education, you can explore the available resources using the Library search and subject filters.

Visit the MASTERBOOK homepage to explore the wider educational platform, or use the Library below to discover available learning materials.

Learning Resources Across Education Levels

Primary Education

Primary-level learning resources support foundational knowledge, literacy, numeracy, subject understanding and the development of essential learning competencies.

Junior School

Junior school resources support learners as they develop knowledge, practical skills and subject understanding across the learning areas offered within the Kenyan CBC framework.

Senior School

Senior school materials support deeper subject learning, revision and preparation for further academic pathways. Learners can explore available notes, coursebooks and other educational materials.

University Education

University resources support independent study, academic development, subject research and deeper engagement with specialised areas of knowledge.

Professional Learning

Professional learning materials support continuing education, knowledge development and the acquisition of skills relevant to professional practice.
